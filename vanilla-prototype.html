Prototype Evaluation Canvas

Requirement 1

User Interaction Point: Where does the user touch or operate the solution?

Primary Physical & Digital Touchpoint:

Touchpoint 1

Symptom Filter Chips

Top triage bar: Patient taps 1 of 5 visual category chips (e.g., Heart / Chest or Joint Pain). Instantly filters doctor list without typing medical jargon.

Touchpoint 2 (Primary)

Interactive Slot Chips (48px Touch Targets)

Located on the Doctor Card: Patient taps a specific time chip (e.g., 10:30 AM [Next Available]). Provides instant tactile green highlight & activates the lock button.

Touchpoint 3

1-Tap "Lock Appointment" CTA

Direct trigger right beneath the slots. Opens the 2-field modal (Name + Phone), requiring under 15 seconds to complete.

Ergonomic & Cognitive Design: Targets are designed with ≥48px hit areas matching the mobile "thumb-zone", removing cognitive load for trembling, sick, or elderly patients.
Requirement 2

Core Function: Which single element addresses the user's difficulty? (Circled & Labelled)

CIRCLED & LABELLED IN PROTOTYPE

The "1-Click Instant Slot-Lock & Live OPD Queue Indicator"

User's Primary Difficulty / Pain Point:

"I am in pain or anxious. When I call the hospital, I am put on hold for 15 minutes. When I visit existing hospital websites, I must fill out a 4-page registration form, only to be told at the end that the doctor has no slots today."

How This Single Element Directly Solves It:

  • Zero-Friction Visibility: Unlocks real-time doctor availability and live queue wait times directly upfront without requiring login or form filling.
  • Live OPD Queue Transparency: Displays "4 patients ahead in Cabin 204 • Avg wait: ~12 mins", removing the terrifying unknown of hospital waiting rooms.
  • Immediate Slot Reservation: One tap locks the appointment slot and bypasses hospital reception queues entirely.
Requirement 3

Knowing Uncertainty: What is the one thing we do not yet know will work?

⚠️
The Critical Leap-of-Faith Hypothesis:

"Will distressed patients trust an automated 30-second digital appointment reservation without demanding a telephone confirmation from a human hospital receptionist, or will no-show rates be unsustainably high (>40%)?"

Why We Don't Know Yet (The Risk):

In healthcare, stakes are emotional and physical. Patients fear turning up at the hospital only to hear "Your doctor had an emergency surgery" or "Our computer didn't register your booking." If they don't trust the automated token, they will still call the front desk (defeating digital automation) or double-book elsewhere.

Prototype Testing Mechanism:

This prototype tests trust by immediately delivering a Digital OPD Token (#OPD-B14), real-time cabin number (Room 204), and a simulated SMS reassurance alert with live clinic navigation link.

Validation Metrics & Success Criteria:
  • Metric A: >80% of test participants report confidence that the doctor will see them without making a phone call.
  • Metric B: <15% no-show rate during the pilot phase with automated SMS reminders.
Pivot / Contingency if False:

If users still demand human voice reassurance, add a 1-tap "Request Instant 60-Sec Receptionist Callback Confirmation" button onto the success screen.
